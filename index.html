OpenRoots Search

One box, every open genealogy source. Live results from free public APIs, one-tap prefilled searches everywhere else. No account, no cookies, nothing stored on our side.

Live results from open APIs

How it works: this page queries only official, free, public endpoints (Internet Archive, Wikipedia, Wikidata, Open Library, Wikimedia Commons, Google Books) directly from your browser. Every other source is opened as a prefilled link on that site's own page, so logins and bot protection stay on their side.

Privacy: queries never touch our servers; nothing is logged, tracked, or stored. Do not use this to search for living people. FamilySearch results need a free account on their site; we never ask for or handle any credentials.

Source quality: volunteer-built sites (Find a Grave links, hobbyist trees) are leads, not proof. Verify against primary records: scrip, census, parish registers, vital stats.
